Questions

Frequently asked

Why do you ask for my location before booking?

Sharing your live location gives the technician precise directions and lets us confirm the real ETA to your doorstep, instead of relying on a general area guess.

What is the average technician ETA?

25 minutes for most of our coverage zones. JP Nagar, Kasavanahalli, Bellandur and MG Road are further from our operating base, so ETA there is closer to 45 minutes.

Do you use genuine spare parts?

Yes — every wheel, handle, zip, runner and lock we fit is a genuine or manufacturer-grade part, backed with a warranty card issued on the day of repair.

Can I book same-day?

Same-day service is available subject to technician availability in your zone — WhatsApp us for the fastest confirmation.
